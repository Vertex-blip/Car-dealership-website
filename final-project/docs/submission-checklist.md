- [ ] Desktop, tablet and mobile layouts show readable content without overlapping or cut-off controls.
- [ ] Both themes have readable contrast; theme choice persists across pages and after reload.
- [ ] Sign-up, rejected incorrect credentials, successful log-in, profile display and log-out work with invented demo details.
